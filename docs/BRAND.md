# Rondacobro — working brand

Decision date: 2026-10-06 (America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires).

## Name and positioning

Working commercial name: **Rondacobro**.

Spanish tagline: **Tus cobros, en orden.**

Descriptor: **Seguimiento de facturas pendientes para agencias y consultoras B2B.**

Rationale: the name combines a repeatable review round with the customer's collection workflow. It is readable in Spanish, points to the proposed function, and supports a calm business tone. The name does not establish a legal entity, registered trademark or available domain.

Use the name in future prepared commercial materials as an experiment. Retain the cobro-agent repository name and existing Netlify URL until a separate, reviewed migration is justified. This decision does not change the deployed UI, activate capture or imply that the paid pilot is available.

## Preliminary name research

Checked 2026-10-06 through two public search engines. Queries included the exact Rondacobro spelling and the spaced Ronda Cobro variant with software. No relevant exact commercial use appeared in the returned results. Results were sparse and sometimes unrelated: absence in search results is not clearance or a guarantee of availability. Domain availability, social handles and official trademark checks remain unverified; no registration, purchase or application was made.

Two earlier candidates were rejected after identifiable overlap: Cobrivo already describes a collections/reminder product at https://www.cobrivo.com/es/ ; Nuvo describes an AI order-to-cash product at https://nuvo.com/ . These are vendor descriptions, not independently verified traction or operational claims. This research supplies competitor leads for later differentiation work, not evidence that our offer will sell.

## Commercial copy rules

- Describe the current product as a fictional-data demo and the paid offer as an unavailable hypothesis until its documented gates pass.
- Do not promise recovered money, time savings, legal collection, guaranteed payment or unattended operation before verification.
- Explain proposed customer value through a clear review queue, reminder preparation and stopping follow-up after payments/disputes/promises.
- Preserve the pilot hypothesis and acceptance threshold in docs/PILOT.md. Branding does not count as demand validation.

## Next evidence

Complete the capture prerequisites, test three distinct qualified businesses' explicit acceptance of proposed price/scope and then verify an actual payment once operational/payment prerequisites are ready. No customers, payments or paid-account access are invented by this naming decision.
